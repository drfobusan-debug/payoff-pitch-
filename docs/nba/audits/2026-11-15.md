## 1. Structural read (why every board is ~50% W-L)

Almost every priced row is stamped **`no_model`** (58,118 season / 16,300 last week) — the rating model has weight **w = 0** until the holdout close test clears (`docs/nba/close_test_rule.md`) **[CODE]**.

So the “board” cuts are the **market’s own two-way book**, not a model lean. Perfect 50/50 W-L and ROI ≈ −juice is the expected null. Last-week Pass tier: **−5.9% ROI** (CI entirely negative). Season Pass: **−6.3%**. That is not a failed model — it is the cost of taking posted prices with no departure.

**Insight:** Do not judge NBA progress on board ROI until `no_model` lifts on at least one market. The scoreboard that matters pre-model is: integrity, over-bias fit, prop band vs-fair, schedule bands, and close-test slope.
