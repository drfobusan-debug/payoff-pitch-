## 6. Why zero live buys?

By design, live = **totals only**, exec = **DK/BetMGM**, and `p_model` is consensus-anchored with **blend weights prior 0** until MDE **[DESIGN]**. With `min_edge` 0.02 and `k=1.0` vs sampling SE, a near-market model rarely clears a buy. Gate funnel still produces live *rows*; they die at edge / thin_market / price band / exposure before `is_buy`.

So empty buys are **expected** in week 1–2 of a prior-zero blend, not proof that totals are dead forever. Separately, the flat board says **Over is losing** — if buys do appear, bias them away from Over until that refusal lifts.
